# iLovu Content Pack

100 new date-idea cards + 100 deep connection questions. Written to match your existing tone (warm, real, anti-pressure, no cheesy therapy-speak). Themed so the cards double as fuel for premium special-date packs later.

**How to use:** Hand this whole file to Claude Code with:
> "Add these to SampleCards.swift and ConnectionQuestions.swift, matching the existing struct shape. For each card, pick a fitting emoji, difficulty (micro/quick/halfDay/fullDay), cost (free/low/medium), and category (cosy/foodie/adventure/creative/intimate) based on its description. For the questions, just append the strings to the existing array so the day-of-year rotation now cycles through ~130 questions."

## PART 1 — 100 DATE CARDS

Organized by theme. Each is a title + one warm sentence of description.

### Cosy Night In (free–low, micro–quick)
1. **Blanket Fort Cinema** — Build a fort with every cushion you own and watch the film one of you has been "meaning to show" the other for years.
2. **Two-Person Bake-Off** — Same recipe, separate bowls, no helping. Judge each other's results with brutal honesty and a kiss.
3. **Candlelit Floor Picnic** — Move dinner to a blanket on the living room floor, lights off, candles only.
4. **The Reverse Day** — Breakfast for dinner, pyjamas all evening, dessert first. Do the whole night backwards.
5. **Handwritten Letter Swap** — Write each other a letter by hand, seal it, swap, read in silence.
6. **Old Photos, New Wine** — Open a bottle and scroll back through every photo of you two from the very beginning.
7. **Living Room Camping** — Sleeping bags, fairy lights, phone torches as "stars," ghost stories optional.
8. **Cook Without a Recipe** — Open the fridge, pick five things, invent a dish together. No looking anything up.
9. **The Massage Trade** — Ten minutes each, no talking, just hands and quiet music.
10. **Playlist Confessions** — Each build a 5-song playlist of "songs that remind me of you," then explain every choice.

### Foodie (low–medium, quick–halfDay)
11. **Order in a Language You Don't Speak** — Find a restaurant whose menu you can't read and order purely by pointing and hoping.
12. **The Tenner Dinner Challenge** — Each given a tenner, separate shops, meet back home and combine whatever you bought into one meal.
13. **Dessert Crawl** — Skip dinner entirely. Visit three places and order only the pudding at each.
14. **Recreate Your First-Date Meal** — Find or cook exactly what you ate the first time you went out.
15. **Blind Taste Test** — Buy five versions of one thing (chocolate, cheese, crisps). Blindfold, rank, argue.
16. **Cook Your Grandmother's Recipe** — One of you teaches the other a dish from your family. Phone home if you have to.
17. **Street Food Hunt** — Pick a market and share one thing from every stall that catches your eye.
18. **The Spice Dare** — Cook one dish, add increasing heat, see who taps out first.
19. **Breakfast in a New Town** — Drive somewhere you've never had breakfast and find the busiest café.
20. **Make Pasta From Scratch** — Flour, eggs, a rolling pin, low expectations, high laughter.

### Adventure & Outdoors (free–medium, halfDay–fullDay)
21. **Coin-Flip Road Trip** — At every junction, flip a coin: heads left, tails right. Drive for an hour. See where you land.
22. **Sunrise You'll Regret Setting an Alarm For** — Pick a high spot, set a 5am alarm, bring coffee in a flask.
23. **The Unplanned Train** — Go to the station, take the next train leaving, get off when somewhere looks interesting.
24. **Wild Swim** — Find a lake, river, or sea spot and get in. Scream together about the cold.
25. **Map Pin Dart** — Drop a pin somewhere within an hour's drive without looking, then go there.
26. **Golden Hour Walk** — Plan a walk that ends at the best view exactly as the sun goes down.
27. **Forage and Cook** — Pick blackberries, wild garlic, or whatever's in season, then cook with it.
28. **Stargazing Drive** — Get away from city lights with a blanket and an app that names what you're looking at.
29. **The Tourist in Your Own City** — Do the most obvious tourist thing in your town that you've never bothered to do.
30. **Cycle to Nowhere** — Two bikes, no destination, turn back when one of you gets hungry.

### Creative & Playful (free–low, quick–halfDay)
31. **Paint Each Other** — Cheap paints, two canvases, ten-minute portraits. Frame the worst one.
32. **Write a Song About Today** — Doesn't matter if it's terrible. Three verses about your day, performed seriously.
33. **The Pottery Disaster** — Air-dry clay on the kitchen table. Make each other a "gift." Keep it forever.
34. **Build Something Flat-Pack** — Get the cheapest flat-pack furniture, no arguing allowed, time yourselves.
35. **Two-Person Photoshoot** — One photographs, one poses, then swap. Find your most ridiculous and your most beautiful shot.
36. **Invent a Cocktail** — Name it after your relationship. Write the recipe down. Make it your "house drink."
37. **The Jigsaw Race** — Two small jigsaws, start at the same time, loser does the washing up.
38. **Draw Your Future House** — Each sketch the home you'd build together, then merge the two into one.
39. **Make a Zine About Us** — Fold paper into a tiny booklet, fill it with drawings and inside jokes.
40. **Lip-Sync Battle** — One song each, full commitment, phone on a tripod, no mercy.

### Intimate & Connection (free, micro–quick)
41. **Eye Contact for Four Minutes** — Set a timer, sit knee to knee, just look. It gets weird, then it gets real.
42. **The Gratitude Round** — Take turns naming one thing you're grateful for about the other, until you run out.
43. **Slow Dance, No Occasion** — One song, kitchen floor, no reason. Just because.
44. **Read to Each Other in Bed** — One chapter of a book, out loud, lights low.
45. **The "Remember When" Game** — Trade your favourite memories of each other until one of you cries (happy tears).
46. **Phone-Free Dinner** — Both phones in a drawer in another room. Just talk. See how long it really lasts.
47. **Trace and Tell** — Draw a slow line down your partner's arm with a fingertip and tell them one thing you love about them.
48. **The Closeness Questions** — Work through a few of the famous get-closer questions over tea.
49. **Bath and Talk** — Run a bath, no agenda, just float and chat.
50. **Make a Wish List for Us** — Each write five things you want to do together this year, then read them aloud.

### Rainy Day / Low Energy (free–low, micro–quick)
51. **The Duvet Day** — Cancel everything, stay in bed, order food, watch a whole series.
52. **Puzzle and Podcast** — A big jigsaw and a podcast you both like. Quiet, easy, together.
53. **Build the Ultimate Hot Drink** — Hot chocolate with everything: cream, marshmallows, a sneaky shot.
54. **Window-Watch the Storm** — Two chairs at the window, a blanket, watch the rain do its thing.
55. **Learn One Card Trick Each** — Ten minutes of online tutorials, then perform for each other.
56. **The Nap Date** — Yes, a nap counts. Curl up, set no alarm, that's the whole date.
57. **Reorganise One Drawer Together** — Oddly satisfying, weirdly bonding, find something you forgot you owned.
58. **Indoor Picnic Under the Table** — Recreate childhood. Crackers, cheese, juice in wine glasses.
59. **Two-Player Game Night** — Co-op a video or board game. Blame each other loudly when you lose.
60. **Face Mask and Trash TV** — Skincare neither of you understands and the worst reality show you can find.

### Special Occasion / Milestone (premium-pack candidates)
61. **The Anniversary Map** — Pin every place that mattered in your relationship and revisit one.
62. **Recreate Your First Date** — Same place, same order, same outfits if you can remember them.
63. **Open the Time Capsule** — Write letters to each other, seal them, plan to open them in exactly one year.
64. **The Year in Review Night** — Look back at everything you did this year together and pick your top three moments.
65. **Renew Your Tiny Vows** — Not married? Make up your own vows for the next year and say them anyway.
66. **The Birthday Surprise Swap** — Each plan a 2-hour surprise for the other on the same day.
67. **Revisit Where You Said "I Love You"** — Go back to the exact spot. Say it again.
68. **The Future Letter** — Write to your relationship five years from now. Hide it somewhere you'll find it.
69. **Celebrate a Half-Anniversary** — Why wait for the full year? Mark six months with something small and silly.
70. **The Milestone Feast** — Cook the most ambitious meal you've ever attempted to mark something big.

### Out & About (low–medium, halfDay)
71. **Gallery Whisper Game** — At a museum, make up the real story behind each painting in whispers.
72. **The Charity Shop Challenge** — A fiver each, find the other the most "them" item in the shop.
73. **Bookshop Blind Date** — Each pick a book for the other based on the cover alone. Buy it. Read it.
74. **Farmers Market Breakfast** — Build breakfast entirely from market stalls, eat it on a bench.
75. **The Free Things Tour** — A whole day out spending nothing: parks, libraries, free museums, window shopping.
76. **Botanical Garden Slow Walk** — No phones, no rush, find the weirdest plant and name it after each other.
77. **Live Music, Any Genre** — Find the smallest gig in town and go, even if it's a genre neither of you knows.
78. **The Viewpoint Picnic** — Find the highest accessible point near you and eat lunch looking down on everything.
79. **Vintage Cinema Trip** — Find somewhere showing an old film and treat it like an event.
80. **Harbour or River Walk at Dusk** — Walk by water as the lights come on, talk about nothing important.

### Flirty & Tender (free, micro–quick)
81. **The Compliment Volley** — No repeats, back and forth, until one of you blushes too hard to continue.
82. **Slow Dance With the Lights Off** — One song, no phone, no talking.
83. **The "What I First Noticed" Reveal** — Tell each other the very first thing you noticed about them.
84. **Kiss for Every Green Light** — On a drive or walk, every green light earns a kiss.
85. **The Truth-or-Truth Game** — No dares, only truths, increasingly bold.
86. **Write Three Things You Find Irresistible** — Swap the notes. No reading aloud. Just keep them.
87. **The Long Goodnight** — No phones in bed for one full night. Just each other.
88. **Recreate Your First Kiss** — Same energy, same nervousness, do it again like it's new.
89. **The Slow Morning** — Set no alarm. Whoever wakes first makes the coffee. Stay in bed regardless.
90. **Two-Minute Heartbeat** — Lie still, hand on each other's chest, feel the rhythm slow down together.

### Try Something New (low–medium, halfDay–fullDay)
91. **Beginner's Class, Anything** — Pottery, dance, climbing, whatever has a "first timers" session this week.
92. **Learn Each Other's Hobby** — Spend an hour being a total beginner at the thing your partner loves.
93. **The New Cuisine Rule** — Eat food from a country neither of you has tried. Order the thing you can't pronounce.
94. **Plant Something Together** — A herb pot, a tree, a window box. Watch it grow as long as you're together.
95. **The Skill Swap** — Each teach the other something you can do that they can't, in 30 minutes.
96. **Volunteer for an Afternoon** — Do something good for someone else, together, for a few hours.
97. **The Language Hour** — Both learn ten words of a new language and only use those words for an hour.
98. **Build a Bucket List Board** — A physical board of everything you want to do together. Hang it where you'll see it.
99. **Try the Thing You're Both Scared Of** — Heights, deep water, public dancing. Hold hands. Do it anyway.
100. **The Reverse Bucket List** — Instead of new things, redo your favourite thing you've ever done together.

## PART 2 — 100 DEEP CONNECTION QUESTIONS

Warm, specific, real. No cheesy therapy language. Append these to your existing 30 so the day-of-year rotation cycles through ~130.

### Light & Warm
1. What's one small thing I did recently that made you happy and I probably don't even know about?
2. If we could wake up anywhere in the world tomorrow, where would it be?
3. What's a memory of us that always makes you smile when it pops into your head?
4. What song instantly reminds you of me?
5. What's something tiny I do that you find weirdly adorable?
6. If we had a completely free Saturday with no plans and no budget limit, what would you want to do?
7. What's the best meal we've ever shared?
8. What's a place we've been that you'd happily go back to a hundred times?
9. What did you think of me the very first time we met — honestly?
10. What's something you've never told me about our first date?

### Getting Deeper
11. When did you most recently feel really loved by me?
12. What's something you wish I asked you about more often?
13. What's a fear you have that you don't talk about much?
14. What does a "good day" look like for you lately?
15. When do you feel most like yourself around me?
16. What's something you're proud of that you don't think I fully realise?
17. What's a way I could support you better right now?
18. What's been on your mind lately that you haven't said out loud?
19. When was the last time you felt genuinely peaceful?
20. What's something you needed as a kid that you still need now?

### Us, Specifically
21. What's something about our relationship you're grateful we figured out?
22. What's a moment you knew you wanted to keep choosing me?
23. What do you think is our greatest strength as a couple?
24. What's something we used to do that you miss?
25. What's a habit we've built together that you love?
26. When have you felt proudest to be with me?
27. What's something you want us to be braver about?
28. What does "home" feel like to you — and is it a place or a person?
29. What's a small ritual of ours that means more to you than I might think?
30. What's something you'd never want us to lose?

### Dreams & Future
31. What's something you want us to do together before we get old?
32. Where do you picture us in five years — and how do you feel about it?
33. What's a dream of yours I could help make happen?
34. If money were no object, what would you want our life to look like?
35. What's something you want to learn or try that you've been putting off?
36. What kind of older couple do you hope we become?
37. What's a tradition you'd love us to start?
38. What's somewhere you've always wanted to take me?
39. What's a goal you have that you've never said out loud to me?
40. What would make the next year of us feel like a good one?

### Tender & Vulnerable
41. When do you feel most secure with me?
42. Is there anything you've been wanting reassurance about?
43. What's a way I've grown since we got together?
44. What's something hard you went through that shaped who you are?
45. When was the last time you cried, and what was it about?
46. What's a part of yourself you're still learning to accept?
47. What do you need more of from me, gently?
48. What's something you forgive me for that I might still feel guilty about?
49. What makes you feel safe?
50. What's a moment you felt closest to me, even if I didn't notice?

### Playful & Fun
51. If you had to describe our relationship as a film genre, what would it be?
52. What's the most ridiculous argument we've ever had?
53. If we swapped bodies for a day, what's the first thing you'd do?
54. What's a nickname for me you've thought of but never used?
55. What's my most annoying habit — and which one do you secretly find endearing?
56. If we started a business together, what would it be and who'd be the boss?
57. What's the weirdest thing about me that you love?
58. If you could relive one day with me, which would it be?
59. What's a "couple thing" other people do that we'd never be caught doing?
60. What's the pettiest thing you've ever been right about in this relationship?

### Past & Story
61. What's your favourite story to tell people about us?
62. When did you first realise you were falling for me?
63. What's something from your past you wish I'd been there for?
64. What did you imagine love would be like before you met me — and how was it different?
65. What's a moment early on when you almost gave up, and what made you stay?
66. What's the best advice about love anyone's ever given you?
67. What's a version of you from the past you wish I'd known?
68. What's something your family taught you about love — good or bad?
69. What's the first thing you ever told a friend about me?
70. If our story had a title, what would it be?

### Everyday Closeness
71. What's the best part of your normal day with me in it?
72. What's something I could do tomorrow that would make your week better?
73. How do you most like to be comforted when you're stressed?
74. What's your favourite way we say "I love you" without saying it?
75. What's something you appreciate about our daily life that's easy to overlook?
76. When do you feel most connected to me during an ordinary week?
77. What's a small kindness I do that you'd miss if it stopped?
78. What does your ideal lazy day with me look like?
79. What's something you'd love to do together more often?
80. What makes you feel like we're a team?

### Growth & Honesty
81. What's something we should probably talk about more than we do?
82. Where do you think we could understand each other better?
83. What's a way you've changed because of me — for better or worse?
84. What's something you're working on in yourself right now?
85. How can we argue better, do you think?
86. What's a fear you have about us that you'd feel relieved to say out loud?
87. What do you think I misunderstand about you?
88. What's a compromise we've made that you're actually grateful for?
89. What would "growing together" look like to you this year?
90. What's something you want me to know but find hard to say?

### Tender Closers
91. What's the kindest thing I've ever done for you?
92. When you picture growing old, what part of it with me are you most looking forward to?
93. What's something you hope never changes about us?
94. If I could feel exactly how much you love me for one minute, what would it feel like?
95. What's a quiet moment with me you'll never forget?
96. What do you love about who you are when you're with me?
97. What's something you want to thank me for that you've never said?
98. If today were a snapshot of us, what would you title it?
99. What's the thing you most want me to remember on a hard day?
100. After all of it — what makes you still choose us?
