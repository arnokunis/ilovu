SIGN-IN FOR REVIEW

iLovu offers Sign in with Apple, which the review team cannot complete, so we
have provided a demo account that signs in with an email and password.

The email option is public and needs no special gesture. On the first screen
("Sign in to save the places you like and pick up right where you left off"),
tap the white "Continue with Email" button directly below the Apple button.
Choose the "Sign in" tab in the sheet that opens, then enter:

  Email:    appreview@ilovu.app
  Password: ilovu123

USING THE APP WITHOUT A PARTNER

iLovu works fully on its own. The demo account is deliberately not connected to
a partner, so the app opens on the "Near You" tab and the bottom tabs read
"Near You", "Saved", "You". Nothing in this review requires a second person or
a second device.

REACHING THE SUBSCRIPTION PAYWALL (Guideline 3.1.2)

The demo account is NOT subscribed. The paywall arms once two places have been
saved, so please save two places first:

  1. Sign in with the demo account. The app opens on the "Near You" tab.
     If asked for location permission, either answer is fine — the deck falls
     back to a default city when location is unavailable.
  2. Swipe RIGHT on two places (or tap the heart). Each one is saved and a
     "Saved to your plans" confirmation appears.
  3. Open the "Saved" tab.
  4. Tap either saved place. The subscription paywall appears immediately.

The paywall shows, in the app itself:
  - The title and length of each plan — "Annual" (auto-renews yearly) and
    "Monthly" (auto-renews monthly)
  - The price of each plan, both visible upfront with nothing behind an extra tap
  - "Cancel anytime" alongside each plan
  - Working links to the Terms of Use / EULA (https://ilovu.io/terms) and the
    Privacy Policy (https://ilovu.io/privacy) in the footer, next to Restore

Either purchase completes in the Apple sandbox environment and unlocks Premium,
or the paywall can be dismissed with the X in the top corner. The in-app
purchases are not restricted by storefront or device.

The App Description also contains a working Terms of Use (EULA) link, and the
Privacy Policy URL is set in App Store Connect.

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))

Account deletion is available in the app:

  1. Sign in (the demo account, or a new account created with an email).
  2. Open the "You" tab — the rightmost tab. It is labelled "Us" once a partner
     is connected; on the demo account it reads "You".
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account".
  4. A confirmation alert explains exactly what will be deleted.
  5. Confirming permanently deletes the account and its data and returns to the
     Sign In screen.

This is a full deletion, not a deactivation. No email, phone call or website
visit is required.

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS VERSION

iLovu was previously a couples app that required connecting with a partner
before it was useful. In 1.2.0 the place discovery, saving and planning features
all work for a single user, and the couples features appear only after a partner
is connected. This is why the first tab, the tab names and the onboarding
screens differ from the previous version.
