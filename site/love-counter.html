Frequently asked

What is a love counter?

A love counter (or relationship counter) counts how long you and your partner have been together. Enter the day you got together and it shows the exact number of days, weeks, months and years since — and how close you are to your next milestone.

How do you count how many days you've been together?

Count the days from the day you got together to today. The counter above does it instantly — just pick your start date. iLovu also keeps a live Days Together count on your phone's home screen as a widget.

What relationship milestones should couples celebrate?

Popular ones are 100 days, 6 months, 1 year, 500 days, 1,000 days, and every yearly anniversary. Marking the small ones — not just the big round numbers — is what keeps a relationship feeling appreciated.
