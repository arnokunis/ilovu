Frequently asked

What should you do on a second date?

Something a step more active and personal than the first — cook together, play mini golf, hit a trivia night, or wander a market. Doing an activity beats another sit-down drink for actually getting to know someone.

Is the second date important?

Often more than the first. The first proves there's a spark; the second shows whether you enjoy actually doing something together. Pick something with a shared activity so there's no pressure to fill silences.
