Would You Rather Questions for Couples

A quick, fun way to find out how in sync you two really are. Take turns, both answer, and see if you match — the fun is when you don't. Perfect for a car journey, a lazy morning, or filling a wait for the bill.

How to play

Both of you answer each one — out loud, at the same time if you can. Matching is sweet; mismatching is funnier, and every "wait, really?" is a tiny thing you didn't know about each other. There's no winning. That's the point.
