Frequently asked

What are good date ideas for married couples?

Ones that reopen real conversation and add a little novelty: a big-questions night, a phone-free dinner, the 36 questions, or trying something neither of you has done. Long-term couples drift from routine, not from lack of love.

How do married couples keep things fresh?

Protect regular one-on-one time, keep asking new questions, and do occasional novel activities together. Small, consistent effort beats one grand gesture a year.
