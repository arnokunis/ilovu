Frequently asked

What's a meaningful anniversary date idea?

The personal ones land hardest: recreate your first date, write time-capsule letters, or revisit the places that mattered. Meaning beats money on an anniversary.

What can couples do for a cheap anniversary?

Recreate your first-date meal at home, look through old photos, or renew a few tiny vows. Almost free, and more memorable than a pricey dinner.
