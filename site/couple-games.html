Couple Games You Can Play Right Now

Three free games for two, playable in this page — no app, no sign-up, no account. You only need one phone between you: one answers in secret, passes it over, and you reveal together. Five minutes, and you'll learn something.

Would You Rather

120 this-or-that dilemmas, the same bank the iLovu app uses. You both answer every one; matching means you're in sync, and mismatching is where the conversation starts.

How Well Do You Know Me?

The same dilemmas, played the other way round. One of you answers for yourself in secret; the other has to guess what they picked. Points go to the guesser, and you swap every round, so you find out who really pays attention.

Never Have I Ever

54 confessions, all of them harmless. You both answer honestly and in secret — the rounds where you disagree are the ones worth talking about.

How to play any of them

All three use the same shape, borrowed from how iLovu works on two phones: answer in secret, then reveal together. That single rule is what makes these different from reading a list of questions aloud. When you can see your partner's face as they answer, you both drift toward the answer you think is expected. Hiding the choice until you've both committed is what makes the reveal worth anything.

Nothing is stored and nothing is sent anywhere. Your names stay in your own browser so you don't have to retype them, and that's the extent of it.

iLovu has a daily Would You Rather and a daily question built in — you each answer on your own phone, whenever you get a minute, and the reveal unlocks once you've both replied. Plus 190+ date ideas for when you're ready to actually go out.

12 more couple games that need nothing but you two

💬The 36 questions

The 36 questions that make two people fall in love — take turns, go in order, don't skip. The only game on this list with peer-reviewed evidence behind it.

🎭Two truths and a lie

Three statements, one false. Even years in, you'll still get caught out — and the ones you get wrong are the interesting ones.

💍The newlywed game

"Who's more likely to…" Write your answers separately, reveal together, argue happily about who really leaves the lights on.

🫙The question jar

Fill a jar with questions on folded paper and pull one at dinner every night. The low-effort version of a daily ritual.

🔢20 questions

One of you thinks of something, the other gets twenty yes-or-no guesses. Loser does the dishes.

🃏Card game night

Learn a two-player card game neither of you knows and fumble the rules together. Cribbage and gin rummy both reward a rematch.

📖One-line story

Build a story one sentence each. It always ends somewhere ridiculous, which is the point.

✏️Blind portrait

Draw each other without looking at the paper. Frame the worst one where guests can see it.

🎶Playlist swap

Each build a five-song playlist for the other with a reason for every track. Listen on the next drive.

📸The photo game

Scroll back to a random month in your camera rolls and take turns explaining a photo the other has never asked about.

🗺️Pin the map

Both point at a spot within an hour's drive that you've never been. Go to whichever one you both didn't pick.

🍜Blind taste test

Three versions of the same thing — olive oil, chocolate, instant coffee. Guess which is which. Absurdly competitive.

Frequently asked

What games can couples play together?

Talking games win, because they need nothing but the two of you: would you rather, how well do you know me, never have I ever, two truths and a lie, the 36 questions, and 20 questions. The three at the top of this page are free to play right here.

What is a good game for couples to play on a phone?

Pass-the-phone games, because you only need one device between you. One person answers in secret, hands the phone over, the other answers, and you reveal together. That takes about five minutes and works in a car, a queue, or waiting for a bill.

How do you play Would You Rather as a couple?

One of you is shown a this-or-that dilemma and picks in secret. Pass the phone over, your partner picks too, then both answers are revealed. Matching means you're in sync; mismatching is usually funnier. Keep a tally over ten rounds for a sync score.

Are these couple games free?

Yes — all three run in your browser with no sign-up, no account, and no payment. Nothing you answer is sent anywhere or stored.

What games can long distance couples play?

Read the prompts aloud on a video call and answer at the same time, or screenshot a question and text your answers simultaneously. See long distance date ideas for more. The iLovu app has a daily version where you each answer on your own phone and the reveal unlocks once you've both replied.
