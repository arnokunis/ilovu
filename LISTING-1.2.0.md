# App Store listing — 1.2.0 (the places pivot)

Paste into App Store Connect. Everything below is en-US.
Old listing sold "plan real dates together" and bought counter intent
(`love counter`, `relationship tracker`, `widget`). That is the documented
keyword mismatch — dropped entirely here.

---

## App Name  (30 max — used 26)

    iLovu: Date Ideas Near You

`date ideas` is the one term that ever converted for us: 15% tap-to-install on
€1.50 of €68 August spend. It never got budget because Max Conversions walked
the money to cheaper counter intent. Putting it in the NAME is the highest-weight
ASO slot there is, and it costs nothing.

## Subtitle  (30 max — used 26)

    Places to Go, Things to Do

Two high-volume searches, no word repeated from the name, and it reads as plain
English rather than a keyword string.

## Keywords  (100 max — used 99)

    restaurant,bar,cafe,hike,trail,weekend,night,city,guide,explore,local,couple,plan,trip,spot,map,eat

Single words only — Apple combines them into phrases automatically, so
"night out" would waste a character on the space and buy nothing. Nothing here
repeats the name or subtitle, which would also be wasted.

**Deliberately dropped: `love counter`, `relationship tracker`, `widget`,
`anniversary`, `days together`.** Those are the terms that filled the funnel with
people who wanted a counter and found a venue swiper. Free organic installs are
not free if they churn on day one.

## Description

Good places near you, actually worth going to.

Not a list of everything around you — a short deck of real spots, with what they
cost, so you can decide in a minute instead of reading forty reviews.

FIND SOMEWHERE TO GO

Swipe through places near you — restaurants, bars, cafés, viewpoints, hikes and
trails, picked for quality rather than proximity alone.

Know what it costs. Every place shows its real price range, not vague symbols, so
nothing surprises you at the table.

Filter for the mood — Italian, sushi, Asian, food and drink, arts, outdoors,
nightlife, hikes and trails.

See everything before you commit — photos, this week's opening hours, the
address, and what people actually said.

SAVE AND GO

Anything you like is saved to your list, waiting for when you have a free evening.

Turn a saved place into a plan with a time, a budget and a checklist, then get
directions or call the venue in one tap.

Add it to your calendar so it actually happens.

WORKS ANYWHERE

Search any city before you get there and line up places to go — genuinely useful
in a city you don't know, where the difference between wandering and going
somewhere good is the whole evening.

Or tap "Search here" and it follows you when you travel.

BRING SOMEONE, IF YOU WANT

Send anyone a plan and they see where you're going before they install anything.

Connect with a partner and it becomes shared: places you both like rise to the
top, and you plan together.

Snap a photo when you go and it's kept in a shared Memory Vault — a map of
everywhere you've been, and a recap of your year.

A daily question and a Would You Rather to stay close between plans.

NO PRESSURE

iLovu doesn't nag you, count your streaks, or guilt you into opening it. It makes
saying "let's actually go" the easy part.

Free to start. No account needed to look around.

## Before submitting — check these

**Age rating is 17+ (18+ in Brazil).** Apple has blocked 18+ downloads in Brazil,
Australia and Singapore since 2026-02-24 for un-verified adults — an install leak
that happens before first_open, so it never shows in our funnel. A places app with
no user-generated content, no chat and no profiles has no obvious reason to carry
it. Re-rating is free reach and unblocks three storefronts.
**But do not re-rate below 18+ if the parked singles/dating layer is ever built.**
That decision comes first; the rating follows it.

**Prices activate 4 September** (EUR 3.99 monthly / EUR 19.99 annual). Submitting
before then is fine — review does not transact — but do not screenshot or quote a
price anywhere in the listing, and there is none quoted above.

**After approval:** ASA test at roughly EUR 85 in Italy, exact match,
**Search Match OFF**. Search Match is what found "couple widget" and walked the
whole budget there.
