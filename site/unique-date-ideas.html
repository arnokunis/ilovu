Frequently asked

What are some unique date ideas?

Try the unexpected: a 'yes day', a silent dinner, geocaching, a coin-flip road trip, or meeting as strangers at a bar. Novelty is what makes a date stick in memory.

Why do new experiences make couples closer?

Relationship research (Aron and others) finds that novel, mildly exciting shared activities boost closeness and satisfaction — more than repeating a comfortable routine.
