Frequently asked

Is coffee a good first date?

Yes — a coffee date is low pressure, cheap and easy to leave or extend, which takes the stakes out of a first meet. Pick somewhere with a bit of atmosphere and something to do nearby.

What do you talk about on a coffee date?

Skip the interview. Ask open questions — favourite trips, what they're into lately, the best thing that happened this week. A few questions to ask your partner can carry the whole date.
