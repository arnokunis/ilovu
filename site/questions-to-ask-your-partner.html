Questions to Ask Your Partner to Feel Closer

Couples drift when they stop asking. These questions reopen the good conversations — some playful, some deep — and they work anywhere: a car, a dinner, a slow Sunday.

💬What's a small thing I do that you love?

Naming the little things makes them happen more often.

💬What did you think of me when we first met?

First impressions are a story you rarely get to hear.

💬What's a dream you haven't told me about yet?

There's almost always one. Asking gives it permission.

💬When do you feel most loved by me?

The most useful thing you'll learn all week.

💬What's your favourite memory of us?

Trading memories is how you audit how much you've lived through.

💬What's something you'd like more of from me?

Braver than it sounds, and worth it every time.

💬What scares you about the future?

Sharing a fear is the fastest way to feel less alone in it.

💬What does a perfect ordinary day look like to you?

Not the holiday — the Tuesday. That's the real answer.

💬Who in your life shaped you the most?

You learn who someone is by who made them.

💬What's something you've never told anyone?

Only if you're both ready — but it changes the room.

Frequently asked

What are good questions to ask your partner?

Mix playful and deep: 'what's a small thing I do that you love?', 'when do you feel most loved by me?', and 'what's a dream you haven't told me about?'. For a structured version, try the 36 questions that lead to love.

What questions bring couples closer?

Questions that invite honest self-disclosure — about feelings, fears, dreams and memories. Taking turns and really listening is what builds closeness, not the questions alone.
