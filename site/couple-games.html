Frequently asked

What are good games for couples to play?

Talking games win: would you rather, the 36 questions, two truths and a lie, the newlywed 'who's more likely to' game, and 20 questions. They need nothing but the two of you.

What games can couples play at home or over text?

Would you rather, never have I ever, guess-my-answer and 20 questions all work at home, on a call, or over text. The iLovu app has a daily version so you never run out.
