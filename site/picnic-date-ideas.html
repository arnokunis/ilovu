Picnic Date Ideas (and What to Actually Pack)

A picnic is the perfect low-pressure date — cheap, unhurried, and easy to make feel special. Here are twelve picnic date ideas, plus what to bring so it isn't just sad sandwiches on the grass.

🌅Sunset picnic

A blanket, snacks, and a spot facing west as the light goes gold.

Low cost

🧺Indoor picnic under the table

Rain outside? Blanket on the floor, fairy lights, juice in wine glasses.

Low cost

🏖️Beach picnic

Sand, sea, and a flask of something warm as the day cools.

Low cost

📚Park picnic and a book

Read to each other between bites. The most underrated picnic move.

Low cost

🍰Dessert-only picnic

Skip the meal — bring only puddings and rank them.

🥐Breakfast picnic

Frequently asked

What should you pack for a picnic date?

A blanket, easy finger food (cheese, fruit, bread, something sweet), a drink and cups, wet wipes, and a small speaker. Bonus points for a candle or fairy lights if it runs into the evening.

What food is good for a picnic date?

Things you can eat with your hands and share: a cheese and charcuterie board, fresh bread, grapes and strawberries, dips, and a couple of pastries or a dessert to finish. Keep it simple and shareable.
